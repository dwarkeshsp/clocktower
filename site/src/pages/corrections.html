Belief-score correction

18 November 2026 · The belief charts and replay surveys have been reprocessed. Game results and Elo ratings are unchanged.

Two bugs affected the optional surveys about who players suspected. After the Demon role passed to another player, some analysis scored beliefs against the dead former Demon. The parser also misread answers that put several players on one line, repeated column labels, or put both probabilities in one column. We corrected both and reparsed the saved raw replies.

The corrected curves still show the newer OpenAI models identifying the Demon more confidently later in games. Both labs' averages rise, so the homepage now describes that measured difference rather than saying that Claude towns stop updating.

Every replay's survey data has been regenerated. The red marker now identifies the living Demon at the moment of each survey, and the library's Best-hidden Demon sort uses that player at the last survey. The surveys never enter the players' conversations; the leaderboard, win rates, rating intervals, head-to-head estimates and game transcripts are unchanged.
